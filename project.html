Executive summary

Designed to make hotel decisions clearer.

The project turns fragmented hotel offers into a transparent recommendation that balances price, cancellation terms, location, guest experience, and family suitability.

It is an independent learning project, not a commercial travel company. Its purpose is to deepen understanding of software architecture, data integration, testing, product decision-making, and responsible use of approved travel data.

Completed in v2.2.0

Clearer reports, safer exports.

New reporting capabilities build on existing price analysis and provider status reports without changing recommendation ranking.

Price Intelligence JSON file export

Save the existing JSON report to a new UTF-8 file. Export never overwrites a file, requires an existing parent directory, and is silent on success.

Provider quota activity

Read-only diagnostics distinguish authoritative current UTC-month usage from a bounded recent-event sample. They describe persisted activity, not remaining allowance or quota exhaustion.

Health-age diagnostics

See the ages of persisted provider attempts and successes. Missing, invalid, or future timestamps remain unavailable; ages do not imply a stale threshold or alter provider trust.

Shared reporting serialization

Consistent UTC timestamps and deterministic JSON formatting preserve existing report compatibility. Invalid non-finite numbers are rejected before export file creation.

Problem statement

Hotel shopping is difficult to compare consistently.

Different providers present price, taxes, cancellation rules, occupancy, and room details in inconsistent ways.

01

Price ambiguity

Base rates may exclude taxes, mandatory fees, or other costs.

02

Policy complexity

Refundability and cancellation terms are often hard to compare.

03

Family fit

Occupancy and suitability can vary even when room names look similar.

Responsible data access

Approved access only.
