Executive summary

Designed to make hotel decisions clearer.

The project turns fragmented hotel offers into a transparent recommendation that balances price, cancellation terms, location, guest experience, and family suitability.

It is an independent learning project, not a commercial travel company. Its purpose is to deepen understanding of software architecture, data integration, testing, product decision-making, and responsible use of approved travel data.

Problem statement

Hotel shopping is difficult to compare consistently.

Different providers present price, taxes, cancellation rules, occupancy, and room details in inconsistent ways.

01

Price ambiguity

Base rates may exclude taxes, mandatory fees, or other costs.

02

Policy complexity

Refundability and cancellation terms are often hard to compare.

03

Family fit

Occupancy and suitability can vary even when room names look similar.

Responsible data access

Approved access only.

The project is designed to use documented APIs, approved affiliate feeds, or user-assisted data sources.

It does not scrape websites, automate browsers, bypass CAPTCHAs, or use undocumented private endpoints.

Credentials remain outside source control, and any future commercial use would require appropriate provider approval.
